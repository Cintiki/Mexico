============================================================
SPRITE FRAME RULES
============================================================

All sprite sheets are horizontal strips.

Frame counts are included in filenames:

_4f = 4 frames
_6f = 6 frames
_8f = 8 frames
_12f = 12 frames

Files ending in _static are single-image static poses.

Suggested animation usage:

Idle:
loop slowly.

Victory:
play when player wins a round or game.

Lose:
play when player loses a round.

Out:
show when player is eliminated.

Bus:
show when player is Riding the Bus.

Dice shake / throw / bounce:
play during roll animation, then show final static dice faces.

============================================================
CODING NOTES
============================================================

Use these image paths exactly as listed.

Do not assume missing assets.
Do not rename assets in code.
Do not generate new character art.
Do not replace Gomez with a rat or mouse.
Gomez is a Chihuahua.

Build UI boxes, buttons, panels, selected states, disabled states, and scoreboard rows with CSS.

Use images for:
- logos
- portraits
- dice faces
- icons
- props
- event cards
- sprite sheets
