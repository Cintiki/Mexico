We are building a web-based version of the dice game Mexico with a custom coin, strike, sombrero, and “Ride the Bus” rule set.

The game should be built around these exact rules.

GAME OVERVIEW

Mexico is a dice elimination game played with two six-sided dice.

Players enter their names before the session starts.

Each player begins the session with:

- 3 coins
- 0 strikes
- Active status

Each individual game costs 1 coin per player to enter.

At the beginning of each game, every participating player pays 1 coin into the pot.

Example:
If 4 players enter the game, each pays 1 coin.
The pot becomes 4 coins.
The winner of that game collects the full pot.

A player may only enter a new game if they have at least 1 coin.

The overall session can continue as long as at least 2 players have enough coins to enter another game.

The winner of an individual game is the last active player remaining.

The winner collects the pot.

CORE DICE SCORING

The game uses two six-sided dice during normal play.

Dice are read as a two-digit number, with the higher die first.

Examples:

6 and 5 = 65
6 and 3 = 63
5 and 4 = 54
4 and 1 = 41
3 and 1 = 31

The special roll 2 and 1 is called Mexico.

Mexico is the highest possible roll.

The full ranking from highest to lowest is:

1. Mexico / 21
2. 66
3. 55
4. 44
5. 33
6. 22
7. 11
8. 65
9. 64
10. 63
11. 62
12. 61
13. 54
14. 53
15. 52
16. 51
17. 43
18. 42
19. 41
20. 32
21. 31

Important:
A roll of 2 and 1 is not treated as the lowest number 21.
It is treated as Mexico, the highest roll in the game.

STARTING A NEW GAME

At the beginning of a new game:

1. All participating players pay 1 coin into the pot.
2. Each participating player rolls one die.
3. The player with the highest single-die roll starts the first round.
4. If there is a tie for highest single-die roll, only the tied players roll one die again.
5. Repeat until exactly one starting player is chosen.
6. Play proceeds clockwise from that starting player.

ROUND STRUCTURE

Each round has a starting player.

The starting player may roll two dice up to three times.

The starting player may stop after:

- 1 roll
- 2 rolls
- 3 rolls

The number of rolls used by the starting player becomes the maximum number of rolls every other player may use during that round.

Examples:

If the starting player rolls once and stops, every other player gets only 1 roll.

If the starting player rolls twice and stops, every other player may roll up to 2 times.

If the starting player rolls three times, every other player may roll up to 3 times.

Only the final roll counts.

If a player chooses to roll again, they must roll both dice.

Players cannot keep one die and re-roll the other.

Every active player gets a turn in the round unless they have already been eliminated before their turn.

ROUND WINNER AND ROUND LOSER

After all active players have rolled for the round:

- The highest final score is the round winner.
- The lowest final score is the round loser.

The round loser receives strikes.

Normally, the round loser receives 1 strike.

The round winner starts the next round.

If there is a tie for lowest, resolve the tie using the tie-breaking rules below.

If there is a tie for highest, resolve the tie using the tie-breaking rules below.

TIE-BREAKING RULES

All ties must be resolved using one die only.

Normal two-dice Mexico scoring is not used for tie-breakers.

Tie-breakers are handled with single-die rolls.

Tie for first starting player:

- Only the tied players roll one die.
- Highest single-die roll wins.
- If still tied, only the still-tied players roll one die again.
- Repeat until exactly one player wins the tie.

Tie for lowest roll:

- Only the tied lowest players roll one die.
- Lowest single-die roll loses the round.
- If still tied, only the still-tied players roll one die again.
- Repeat until exactly one loser is found.
- The final loser receives the strike penalty from the round.

Tie for highest roll:

- Only the tied highest players roll one die.
- Highest single-die roll wins the tie.
- If still tied, only the still-tied players roll one die again.
- Repeat until exactly one round winner is found.
- The final winner starts the next round.

No game may end with a shared winner.

If any final tie affects who wins, who loses, who is eliminated, or who starts the next round, resolve it with one-die tie-breakers until there is exactly one result.

MEXICO PENALTY

Mexico is the roll 2 and 1.

Each Mexico rolled during a round increases the strike penalty for the round loser.

The Mexico penalty stacks by adding 2 strikes per Mexico rolled.

Penalty table:

- 0 Mexicos rolled in the round = loser receives 1 strike
- 1 Mexico rolled in the round = loser receives 2 strikes
- 2 Mexicos rolled in the round = loser receives 4 strikes
- 3 Mexicos rolled in the round = loser receives 6 strikes
- 4 Mexicos rolled in the round = loser receives 8 strikes

The penalty is based on the number of Mexicos rolled during the round before tie-breaking.

One-die tie-breaker rolls do not count as Mexico rolls.

Tie-breaker rolls do not increase the Mexico penalty.

Example:

Four players are playing.

Player 1 rolls Mexico.
Player 2 rolls Mexico.
Player 3 rolls Mexico.
Player 4 rolls 65.

Player 4 has the lowest score because Mexico beats every other roll.
There were 3 Mexicos in the round.
The penalty is 6 strikes.
Player 4 receives 6 strikes and is eliminated immediately.

STRIKES

Each player starts each individual game with 0 strikes.

A player is normally eliminated when they reaches 5 strikes.

The strike limit is 5.

RIDE THE BUS

The first player in the game to reach exactly 5 strikes gets one final chance.

This is called Riding the Bus.

That player must wear the Sombrero.

In the game UI, this player should have a visible “Sombrero” or “Riding the Bus” status.

Only one player per game can Ride the Bus.

When the first player reaches exactly 5 strikes:

- They are not eliminated yet.
- Their status becomes Riding the Bus.
- They receive the Sombrero.
- They remain active.
- If they lose another round later, they are eliminated.

Important:
A player only gets to Ride the Bus if they are the first player to reach exactly 5 strikes.

If a player jumps past 5 strikes because of a Mexico penalty, they are eliminated immediately and do not get to Ride the Bus.

Example:

Player has 4 strikes.
They lose a normal round and receive 1 strike.
They now have exactly 5 strikes.
If no other player has already ridden the bus, this player now rides the bus.

Example:

Player has 4 strikes.
They lose a round with 1 Mexico penalty and receive 2 strikes.
They now have 6 strikes.
They are eliminated immediately.
They do not get to Ride the Bus.

If a player reaches 5 strikes after another player has already received the Ride the Bus chance, they are eliminated immediately.

Only the first exact 5-strike player gets the Ride the Bus chance.

ELIMINATION RULES

A player is eliminated if any of the following are true:

1. They reach 5 strikes but another player has already received the Ride the Bus chance.
2. They jump past 5 strikes because of a Mexico penalty.
3. They are already Riding the Bus and lose another round.
4. They are the final loser of a tie-breaker and the resulting strike penalty eliminates them.

Eliminated players no longer roll in the current game.

Eliminated players do not lose additional coins during that game.

They keep any remaining coins for future games.

NEXT ROUND STARTER

The player with the highest final roll in a round starts the next round.

If multiple players tie for highest final roll, resolve the tie with one-die tie-breakers.

The winner of that one-die tie-breaker starts the next round.

If the highest player is eliminated due to some edge case, the next starter should be determined from the remaining active players using a one-die roll-off.

ENDING AN INDIVIDUAL GAME

The game continues until only one active player remains.

The last active player wins the game.

The winner collects the full pot.

Example:

Four players enter a game.

Each pays 1 coin.

Pot = 4 coins.

Adam wins the game.

Adam collects 4 coins.

If Adam had 2 coins after paying the entry fee, he now has 6 coins.

SESSION CONTINUATION

After a game ends:

- Reset all strikes to 0.
- Clear all eliminated statuses.
- Clear Riding the Bus status.
- Remove the Sombrero status.
- Keep each player’s current coin count.
- Players with at least 1 coin may enter the next game.
- Players with 0 coins cannot enter the next game.

The next game begins by collecting 1 coin from each participating player and rolling one die each to determine the first starting player.

EDGE CASES

If a penalty would eliminate multiple players at the same time, apply the strike and elimination rules first.

If only one active player remains after eliminations, that player wins immediately.

If multiple players remain, continue the game.

If a tie affects elimination, resolve the tie with one-die rolls before applying the strike penalty.

One-die tie-breaker rolls never count as normal Mexico rolls.

One-die tie-breaker rolls never affect the Mexico penalty.

All ties must be resolved to one clear player.

There are no shared round winners.

There are no shared round losers.

There are no shared game winners.

IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

Do not treat 21 as a normal low roll.

21 is always Mexico and always the highest roll.

Do not allow players to keep one die and re-roll the other.

Do not count tie-breaker rolls as Mexico rolls.

Do not apply two-dice scoring to tie-breakers.

Do not allow unresolved ties.

Do not allow more than one Ride the Bus player per game.

Do not give Ride the Bus to a player who jumps from below 5 strikes to above 5 strikes.

Do not reset coins between games.

Do reset strikes between games.

Do reset Riding the Bus and Sombrero status between games.

Create a function:

Rules:

if mexicoCountThisRound === 0:
    penalty = 1
else:
    penalty = mexicoCountThisRound * 2

Create a function:

resolveTieWithOneDie(players, tieType)

Rules:

- Only tied players roll one die.
- For lowest tie, lowest die loses.
- For highest tie, highest die wins.
- If tie remains, repeat with still-tied players.
- Return exactly one player.

Create a function:

applyStrikePenalty(player, penalty)

Rules:

- Add penalty to player.strikes.
- If player is already Riding the Bus and receives any new strike penalty, eliminate them.
- If player.strikes === 5 and Ride the Bus has not been used, set player to Riding the Bus and give Sombrero.
- If player.strikes === 5 and Ride the Bus has already been used, eliminate the player.
- If player.strikes > 5, eliminate the player immediately.
